End-to-End Freight Lifecycle

From initial consignment booking to final delivery acknowledgment in four structured stages.

01

Consignment Creation

Shipper specifies product type, cargo weight, required truck configuration, pickup and destination, and attaches commercial invoice & DO.

02

Transporter Allocation

Shipper selects a verified transporter from the accredited directory or posts to the open network pool.

03

Dispatch & Telemetry

Transporter accepts consignment, binds fleet truck & licensed driver, and initiates transit with live GPS updates.

04

Milestone & Delivery

Real-time checkpoint logs, in-app messaging, status progression, and digital delivery receipt for all stakeholders.
